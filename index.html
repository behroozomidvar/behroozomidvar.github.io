About

I am a Science Lead and Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon Web Services (AWS), working on Agentic AI. My current focus is on building memory, context, and personalization systems for AI coding agents.

My research interests span large language models, code generation, AI agents, and interactive data exploration. I have published in top venues including SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, and KDD.

Previously, I worked on data exploration and summarization systems, developing novel approaches to help users understand and interact with large-scale datasets.
